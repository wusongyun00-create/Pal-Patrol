Troubleshooting

How to Fix Pal Patrol Connectivity Issues

Having trouble connecting? Most connection issues can be fixed with a few quick checks.

IN THIS ARTICLECharge your sensorKeep your phone closeCheck BluetoothCheck app permissionsRestart and updatePair your sensor againStill having trouble?

01

Make sure your sensor is charged

Connect your sensor to its charger and make sure it has enough power before continuing.

IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
Sensor charging

02

Keep your phone close

During setup and pairing, keep your phone close to your Pal Patrol sensor.

03

Check Bluetooth

Make sure Bluetooth is turned on in your phone settings.

SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER
Bluetooth settings

04

Check app permissions

Make sure the Pal Patrol app has the required permissions enabled.

SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER
App permissions

05

Close, restart, and update

Fully close the Pal Patrol app, then open it again and try reconnecting. Restart the sensor and wait a few moments before reconnecting. A phone restart can resolve temporary Bluetooth or app issues.

Finally, make sure you are using the latest version of the Pal Patrol app.

SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER
App update

06

Pair your sensor again

In the Pal Patrol app, go to Settings → Devices → Remove Device.

Then select Add Device → Pair Your Pal Patrol.

SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER
Device settings

Still having trouble?

We will help you get back on track.

When contacting support, include your phone model, operating system, Pal Patrol app version, what happens when you try to connect, and any error message you see.
