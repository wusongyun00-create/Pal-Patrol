## Optional images for a production launch

- Collar product photography: front, side, hardware close-up, and collar-on-pet shots against the three band colors. Use a clean cream background for product-detail modules.
- App-store images: 2–3 real screenshots showing the home dashboard, live location, sleep trends, and wellness history. The current CSS phone mockups are design stand-ins, not screenshots.
- Customer stories: individual pet-and-person portraits in natural light, with written permission for marketing use.
- Social/video: short vertical clips of a pet walking, sleeping, or returning home. Keep the feeling observed and intimate rather than surveillance-heavy.

## Replacement method

Each placement has a meaningful selector in `styles.css`: `.hero-image`, `.collar-photo`, `.sleep-image`, `.dog-card .pet-image`, `.cat-card .pet-image`, `.colour-grid`, `.story-photo`, and `.shop-product-photo`. Keep asset replacements in the `assets/` folder beside `index.html`.

Use images you own, have licensed, or that are otherwise cleared for commercial use. The copied source files remain unmodified; visible source marks were not removed or concealed. The source pet-sitting archive was inspected only for broad layout/reference analysis; none of its images or source code is used in this site.
