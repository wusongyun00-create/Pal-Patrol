Pal Patrol App Version 1.0

Meet Pal Patrol, a smarter way to understand your dog or cat each day.

This first release connects your Pal Patrol smart collar with the Pal Patrol mobile app, bringing location, activity, sleep, wellness, and behavior insights into one simple daily view.
