Does Pal Patrol work for dogs and cats?+

Yes. Pal Patrol is designed for both dogs and cats, with comfort, sizing, and everyday movement in mind.

Can Pal Patrol tell me if my pet is sick?+

Pal Patrol provides wellness and behavior insights, not a medical diagnosis. It is designed to help you notice everyday trends to discuss with a veterinarian when needed.

How does the emotion insight work?+

Emotion insights are based on patterns in activity, rest, and routine. They are intended as a helpful trend view, not a definitive reading of your pet’s feelings.

Will I need a subscription?+

Connectivity and selected advanced features may require a Pal Patrol plan. Plans and full pricing should be made clear before checkout.
