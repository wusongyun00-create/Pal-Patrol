Introducing Emotion Insights: A New Way to Understand Your Pet

By Pal Patrol • September 8, 2026

We built Pal Patrol around a simple idea: The better we understand our pets, the better we can care for them.

Today, we’re introducing a new feature designed to help you do just that.

Meet Emotion Insights

Your pet can’t tell you how their day went. But their everyday patterns can tell us a lot.

Emotion Insights uses activity and behavioral information collected through Pal Patrol to create simple visual insights that help you understand your pet’s daily patterns.

Instead of looking at a page full of numbers, you’ll see easy to understand indicators that help you recognize what may be happening throughout your pet’s day.

How to Find Emotion Insights

Open the Pal Patrol app and select your pet. Go to Pet Profile → Insights → Emotion.

How to Read the Insights

ActiveYour pet has been moving more throughout the day.RestingYour pet has spent more time resting or sleeping.RelaxedTheir recent patterns appear consistent with a calmer period.Different from usualTheir recent activity may look different from their normal routine.

Look for Patterns

One day can be interesting. A pattern over time can be much more meaningful.

As you continue using Pal Patrol, you can look back at your pet’s information and learn what their normal routine looks like.

A New Way to Stay Close

Emotion Insights is not about replacing your instincts as a pet parent. It is about giving you another way to understand the companion who can’t use words to tell you what they need.

Because sometimes, paying a little closer attention can mean a lot.
