GUIDE 01

How to wear your sensor

Give your pet a comfortable fit, then let the collar settle into their normal day.

  1. Choose the right fitMake sure the collar is comfortable and secure. You should be able to fit approximately two fingers between the collar and your pet’s neck.
  2. Position the sensorAttach the sensor securely according to the product instructions.
  3. Check the fitMake sure the collar stays comfortably in place while your pet walks, plays, sits, and rests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Quick answers to some of the questions we hear most often.

Why is my sensor not working?+

Don’t worry. There are a few simple things you can check first.

  1. Check the battery Make sure your sensor has enough charge.
  2. Check the connection Make sure Bluetooth is turned on and your phone is close to the sensor.
  3. Restart the sensor Turn the sensor off and back on, then try reconnecting.
  4. Restart your phone A quick restart can sometimes resolve temporary connection issues.
  5. Check for app updates Make sure you are using the latest version of the Pal Patrol app.
  6. Try pairing again Go to Pal Patrol App → Settings → Devices → Pal Patrol Sensor, then select “Reconnect.”

How does the emotion insight work?+

Your pet can’t tell you how they’re feeling, but their everyday behavior can give us helpful clues.

Pal Patrol uses activity and behavioral patterns to create simple visual insights that help you understand changes in your pet’s everyday routine.

Active Higher levels of movement.Resting More time resting or sleeping.Relaxed Patterns consistent with a calmer period.Different from usual Activity that may look different from their typical routine.
IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
Emotion Insights dashboard

Emotion Insights are intended to help you understand patterns, not tell you exactly what your pet is feeling. They do not replace your own observations or professional veterinary care.

Can Pal Patrol tell me if my pet is sick?+

Pal Patrol is designed to help you understand everyday wellness patterns. It can help you notice changes in sleep, activity, movement, and other tracked behaviors.

However, Pal Patrol does not diagnose illnesses or medical conditions.

If your pet is sleeping more than usual, Pal Patrol may help you notice the change, but it cannot tell you why that change is happening.

IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
Wellness trend showing a change over time

If you notice something unusual or have concerns about your pet’s health, contact your veterinarian.

Pal Patrol supports everyday wellness. Your veterinarian provides medical care.
